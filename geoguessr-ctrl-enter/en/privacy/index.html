Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

GeoGuessr Ctrl+Enter Chat (the “Extension”) does not collect, record, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information, chat messages, keystrokes, browsing history, cookies, or any other user data.

Key event processing

In GeoGuessr match chat, the Extension detects Enter, Control+Enter, Command+Enter, and the state of Japanese text conversion. This processing occurs temporarily and only within your browser. The Extension does not log keystrokes or read, store, or transmit the text entered in chat.

Supported site

The Extension runs only on https://www.geoguessr.com/*. It does not run on other websites.

Network access and storage

The Extension makes no external network requests and uses no analytics, advertising, external JavaScript, cookies, or external storage.

Google Tag Manager on this website

This informational website loads Google Tag Manager to manage tags used on the site. Your browser connects to Google servers when a page is displayed. Google Tag Manager is used only by this website and is not included in the Extension. Google’s privacy policy applies to Google’s handling of data.

Third-party service

GeoGuessr is an independent third-party service. GeoGuessr’s privacy policy applies to data handled on GeoGuessr. This unofficial Extension is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to GeoGuessr or its operators.

Policy changes

If this policy changes, the latest version will be published on this page and the last-updated date will be revised.
